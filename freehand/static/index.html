Command Box Alt+C

Voice Alt+V

Shortcuts, from anywhere on the page: Alt+V starts and stops listening, like pressing Speak. Alt+Shift+V turns voice conversation on (it starts listening straight away) or off. Press Speak and wait for the high beep, then talk. Press Speak again when you've finished: a low beep means FreeHand has stopped listening. Your voice is turned into text on this computer, not sent anywhere. While FreeHand reads replies aloud, your screen reader stays quiet so the two don't talk over each other. If you use a screen reader with speakers, try not to move around the page while it's listening: the microphone can hear your screen reader. Headphones stop that completely.

Scribble Pad Alt+S

Your scribble pad is one page of notes that you keep adding to. Type anywhere in it, then press Save or Ctrl+Enter. Your words stay on the pad after saving; that's normal. FreeHand sorts tasks and notes off the pad later, or straight away with Sweep Now.

    Skills

    Skills are instructions FreeHand saves for tasks you do more than once. New skills start as drafts. A draft becomes permanent after it has worked 3 times, or when you approve it. Skills that keep failing, and drafts nobody uses for 30 days, are archived, so the list stays tidy.

            Remote Gateways

            Connectors vs gateways: a connector is an account the agent acts with (e.g. your Slack shared app → post/read channels). A gateway is a channel that reaches you — where approval requests and remote commands arrive (Telegram / Slack / WhatsApp bot tokens below). Different credentials, different jobs. From chat you can approve or deny only requests made from that same chat; destructive actions must be approved here in the console.

            WhatsApp

            Step 1: Choose which number FreeHand uses

            We recommend a separate WhatsApp number for FreeHand, such as a spare SIM or a WhatsApp Business number. It keeps FreeHand's chats apart from your own, so it's cleaner.

            No second number? That's fine. Use your own number, and talk to FreeHand in your "Message yourself" chat. To find it, start a new chat in WhatsApp and pick your own name at the top of the contact list.

            Step 2: Link that number

            Spaces and the plus sign are fine.

            Then, on the phone with that number, open WhatsApp and go to Settings, then Linked devices, then Link a device. Choose "Link with phone number instead" and type the code.

            Step 3: Say who may talk to FreeHand

            One number per line, with country code. FreeHand ignores messages from anyone not on this list. If you used a separate number in step 2, put your own personal number here.

            Leave this on if you linked your own number.
